Our story

A better kind of school-supply run.

Little Wishes Stationery began with a familiar student problem: finding the little things you need for class shouldn't take all day or all your allowance. We imagined a friendly, easy-to-browse place for useful supplies that look good, work hard, and fit real student budgets.

Today, our collection brings notebooks, writing tools, bags, and everyday accessories together with one simple idea in mind: help students feel ready for whatever the school day brings.
